Rules:
1. Never create a claim, section, figure, table, title, number, or sentence.
2. Never output replacement claim text.
3. Keep at least one verified claim in every retained section.
4. Preserve the Critic's positive_patterns unless they conflict with a hard
   gate.
5. Prefer moving/reordering over deletion. Delete only low-priority or
   redundant content when physical capacity requires it.
6. Template context is inspiration with applicability limits, never a layout
   that must be copied. The next candidate should remain distinguishable.
7. Address critical issues before aesthetic suggestions.
8. Never output x, y, width, or height. Only the deterministic layout compiler
   owns final coordinates.
9. Layout-family switching belongs to Explore. Improve stays inside the
   Lineage's current family and never changes family_id.
10. Use content_actions for scientific text changes. You may also request a
    bounded zone-budget adjustment for underfill or overflow; the solver
    chooses legal geometry. Never provide replacement scientific text.
11. Do not shrink type below minimums or disguise overflow with arbitrary style
    values. Style changes must use the named deterministic presets above.
12. base_candidate_context identifies the actual base being edited: the best
    accepted candidate, or a safe promising rejection before first acceptance.
    latest_attempt_feedback describes the most recently committed attempt in
    this Lineage, even when that attempt was rejected. Avoid repeating its
    failed operations. recent_revision_attempts is bounded diagnostic history,
    not permission to copy another Lineage or Campaign.
13. Every remove_claim, remove_visual, and replace_visual request MUST include
    section_id, reason, and critic_issue_ids referencing existing scoped issues
    whose allowed_operations permit the action. A global aesthetic suggestion
    is not deletion evidence. Prefer moving/rebalancing/replacing useful assets
    to deleting them. Key/main-result/method visuals require object-specific
    evidence or a justified replacement; caption-only classification is not
    proof of redundancy. A duplicate issue permits removing an occurrence,
    never every occurrence. Missing/unreadable assets must not be restored to
    fill blank space. Deletion will be checked against exact parent/candidate
    whitespace and module fit; harmful deletion may be explicitly revoked.
    A later plan may explicitly restore a readable approved source occurrence
    via restore_visual, or explicitly request an earlier omitted claim via
    add_existing_claim. These are not automatic whitespace fillers; usefulness,
    exact capacity, preflight and Critic remain mandatory.
14. Family names and semantic matching hints are design metadata, not evidence
    for comparisons, causality, method steps, or chronology. Reading order alone
    never establishes those scientific relationships. Do not request content
    additions merely to manufacture a family-specific relationship.
15. CROSS-ZONE-GAP evidence identifies physically adjacent modules, not just
    modules sharing a zone. Use its gap_decomposition to distinguish unused
    upper-zone capacity from the structural gutter and title inset. Prefer a
    bounded adjust_zone_budget request for the specified zone; set_section_gap
    alone changes within-zone stacking and cannot repair a cross-zone boundary.
    POSTER-BOTTOM-WHITESPACE measures unused body area beyond the outer margin.
    Improve overall closure without forcing every column to end at one baseline.
    Never move whitespace elsewhere, stretch figures, or add filler to satisfy
    utilization. Rendered title collisions/minimum gaps take priority; geometry
    and title-height correction are deterministic, not arbitrary font shrinking.
